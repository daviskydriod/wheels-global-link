# AWA AUTO MALL Website

## Goal
Build a premium, responsive multi-page automotive marketplace for vehicle and spare-parts discovery, with strong WhatsApp and inquiry conversion paths.

## Pages and navigation
- Shared sticky navigation, mobile menu, footer, WhatsApp shortcut, and consistent contact actions.
- Home page with the full requested section sequence: vehicle-led introduction, search prompt, two category features, featured vehicles, parts categories/products, global sourcing, reasons to choose AWA, process, about, inquiry, and contact.
- Dedicated pages for Cars, individual vehicle details, Spare Parts, individual part details, About, Why AWA, and Contact.
- Vehicle and part detail pages will use reusable data objects and dynamic slugs so live inventory can replace placeholders later.

## Visual direction
- Preserve the supplied logo exactly and use it throughout the site and as the basis for the favicon.
- Use royal blue, automotive red, white, and dark navy with sharp editorial layouts, restrained borders, premium vehicle photography, and minimal rounding.
- Provide distinct mobile layouts, touch-friendly actions, scrollable image galleries, and a compact hamburger navigation.
- Use subtle reveal and interaction motion with reduced-motion support.

## Content and interactions
- Add representative placeholder inventory clearly labeled through availability and price treatments, without fabricated reviews, certifications, or statistics.
- Wire telephone, website, TikTok, and WhatsApp actions to their appropriate destinations.
- Validate the inquiry form in the browser and prepare its submission as an encoded WhatsApp message, avoiding unsupported server storage.
- Make vehicle and product filtering/search useful with local structured data.

## Assets
- Store the supplied logo through the project asset flow without altering its visible design.
- Generate a cohesive set of premium automotive, showroom, parts, and global-sourcing imagery where real inventory imagery is unavailable.

## Search and sharing
- Add unique titles, descriptions, Open Graph text, canonical paths, semantic headings, and descriptive image text to every content page.
- Preserve and update crawler access rules. Sitemap creation will remain deferred until the final public domain is connected, so no temporary preview URL is embedded.

## Verification
- Check all pages and navigation links, form behavior, dynamic detail routes, and WhatsApp links.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts visually and confirm the latest preview build has no errors.
